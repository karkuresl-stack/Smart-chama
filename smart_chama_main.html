Main System

Access your Smart Chama workspace using the relevant module below.

Member Access Sign in or access your member account. Group Dashboard View group memberships and member information. Savings & Transactions Submit deposits and view transaction history. Officer Approvals Review pending deposits and take authorised action. Loans & Repayments Apply for loans, review applications when authorized, and view repayment schedules. Administration Open the existing administration page.

Our Platform

Smart Chama is being developed to support multiple chamas, organizations and SACCOs through one central platform. Access to financial records and officer functions must be enforced by the database, not just by hiding links on this page.

Some modules are still under development. Use the relevant pages for their existing functions while the unified login, organization management and role-based navigation are completed.
